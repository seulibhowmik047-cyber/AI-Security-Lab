
# AI Threat Detection

## Objective

The objective is to identify suspicious or malicious activity involving AI applications, AI agents, identities, APIs, resources, and data.

---

## Example

A user normally accesses low-risk business information.

Suddenly, the same identity attempts to access sensitive information at an unusual time.

This may require investigation.
